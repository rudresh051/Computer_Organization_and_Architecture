## What does a Computer Understand?

* Computers do not understand natural human languages, nor programming languages
* They only understand the language of bits

* Bit - 0 or 1  
* Byte - 8 bits
* Word - 4 bytes
* kiloByte - 1024 bytes
* megaByte - 10^6 bytes
